---
description: "Sorting and other helpers over Vec, plus the collection types in its submodules."
---
The collection types live in submodules: [`HashMap`](/packages/stdlib/reference/collections/map/HashMap), [`HashSet`](/packages/stdlib/reference/collections/set/HashSet), [`TreeMap`](/packages/stdlib/reference/collections/tree/TreeMap), [`VecDeque`](/packages/stdlib/reference/collections/deque/VecDeque), [`List`](/packages/stdlib/reference/collections/list/List) and [`BitSet`](/packages/stdlib/reference/collections/bitset/BitSet). `Vec` itself is in the [prelude](/docs/reference/prelude/Vec); its functional helpers (`map`, `filter`, `fold`, …) are in [`collections::vec`](/packages/stdlib/reference/collections/vec).
